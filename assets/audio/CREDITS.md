## Usage and adaptations

- Recorded wooden footsteps, small wood contact and fabric impact: Kenney. Fabric impact is a quiet swat proxy, not a recorded insect being killed.
- Recorded mouse click: quiet UI click and very low-level phone-touch feedback. It is not claimed to be a dedicated smartphone recording.
- Generic recorded clothing rustle: bedding movement. Paper handling: book movement, menu/page handling and research notes.
- Recorded tool ratchet: physical repair completion. No synthetic repair/reward jingle or robotic dialogue bleeps.
- Indoor trailer room tone already includes distant road ambience and electrical presence; it is not a separate new outdoor recording.
- Refrigerator recording: only the CC0 free sample from OpenGameArt, not LEGIT Audio's separately licensed paid library.
- Gain and slight one-shot pitch variation are applied at playback. Loops use two overlapping players and real-time crossfades; recording excerpts are bounded in the audio scripts.

## Not included

No signature music, voice acting, synthesized reward/alert sounds, dedicated electronic robot acknowledgment, bespoke robot hover recording or dedicated phone touchscreen recording. Existing recordings provide restrained foley substitutes where described above; Music routing is prepared for Step 42D.
